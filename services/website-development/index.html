QUESTIONS

The things people ask us first.

Can we edit the website ourselves?

Yes. We build on a CMS for the parts you will want to change, and hand over a short walkthrough so your team can update content without coming back to us.

Will the website rank on Google?

We build the foundations that ranking depends on: clean structure, fast loading, correct metadata, structured data and crawlable content. Ranking for competitive terms also needs ongoing content and SEO work, which we can take on separately.

Do you use WordPress or custom code?

Whichever serves the site. A content-heavy site your team edits daily may be best on a CMS; a fast marketing site or product site is often better custom-built. We recommend based on how you will actually run it.

What do you need from us to start?

Your brand assets if you have them, a sense of who you are selling to, and someone who can approve decisions. We handle structure and copy direction if you do not have them yet.
