QUESTIONS

The things people ask us first.

Do you build for mobile, web or both?

Mobile most often, and web where the game should open without an install. We decide with you early, because it affects the engine and the multiplayer architecture.

Can you add multiplayer to an existing game?

Sometimes, and it depends on how the game handles state. We review the codebase first and tell you honestly whether it is an extension or close to a rebuild.

We need a game for brand engagement or training, not as a product.

That is a common brief and a different one. The mechanics serve the outcome you actually want, whether that is time on brand, a learned behaviour or a measurable score.

What about art and assets?

We handle art direction, UI and integration. Where a project needs specialist illustration, 3D or sound, we bring in the right people and keep the work and the ownership with you.
